The Extended Essay Explorer

You're a curious student wandering the Great Library, hunting for the perfect IB Extended Essay topic. Twelve mentors are scattered about — talk to each one. Some offer a choice of answers; others invite you to reply in your own words. Answer honestly: it all builds a picture of where your curiosity lives.

When you've gathered every clue, the Topic Oracle 🔮 awakens, points you toward a subject area (only ones our school offers), and writes a personalized prompt you can copy straight into your AI Topic Coach to start finding a topic.

Move with WASD / arrow keys (or the on-screen pad). Walk up to a glowing mentor and press E / TALK to speak.
